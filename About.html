About Me

As a UX/UI Product Designer with an education background in industrial design, I specialise in crafting user-centered solutions that drive customer engagement, honed through diverse roles across industries. Currently, I design intuitive experiences and impactful designs within a dynamic team at an insurance company.

My career spans diverse roles, including optimising internal tools as a UX/UI Designer in an IT department, delivering B2B solutions at a tech firm, and driving innovative projects at a startup, where I pitched and designed for a wide range of clients.

I bring expertise in user testing, developing user journeys, and creating experience maps to uncover insights and enhance product interactions. Passionate about staying ahead of digital trends and user needs, I thrive on translating user needs into impactful designs.

For me, designing isn’t just a job - it’s a purpose that fuels my creativity and drives meaningful outcomes.

Side Project

A self-initiated MVP I designed and built on my own, to prove the workflow was worth engineering time.

Design QA was full of manual logistics: finding the right build for each device, passing screenshots through chat, annotating in Figma, re-screenshotting into tickets and tracking 20+ issues by hand. As a designer, not a developer, I built a tool with Claude Code to remove those steps.

  • Figma plugin that exports frames directly into a QA session
  • Web dashboard for side-by-side comparison of design vs build, with annotation
  • P1-P4 issue logging with status tracking
  • QR code that pairs test devices to upload screens
  • Shareable session link so developers can view issues on their own devices, and export to PDF or JSON
  • Deployed with persistent cloud storage (Supabase backend)
